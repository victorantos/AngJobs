Instinct Science - Senior Full-Stack Engineer (Elixir + React) - REMOTE (US)

Instinct Science is the AI leader in veterinary medicine. We build the platform that powers animal hospitals — a cloud-native EMR with real-time clinical decision support and applied AI that helps veterinary teams practice at their best. Thousands of users across the globe rely on what we ship every day.

We&#x27;re at an inflection point: proven product-market fit, scaling fast, and greenfield work ahead that will serve tens of thousands of users. You&#x27;re measured by customer impact, not story points.

WHAT YOU&#x27;LL DO:
- Drive product direction alongside customers and PMs — not just implement specs

WHAT WE&#x27;RE LOOKING FOR:
Must-haves:
- 5+ years building modern SaaS applications, full-stack
- Ability to break complex problems into efficient, actionable work
- Team-first attitude; strong communication in a remote environment
- Empathy for customers — you care about what ships and who uses it

Nice-to-haves:

- TypeScript and React
- AWS (build, deploy, troubleshoot)
- Relational database design and query tuning

STACK: Elixir, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, AWS
