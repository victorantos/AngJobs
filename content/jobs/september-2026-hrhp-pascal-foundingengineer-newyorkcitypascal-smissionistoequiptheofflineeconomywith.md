Pascal - Founding Engineer - New York City
Pascal’s mission is to equip the offline economy with foundational intelligence, starting with the consumer packaged goods (CPG) sector. We’re reworking the archaic data foundation for agents that automate outcomes: recover lost revenue, optimize sales, and proactively resolve operational breakdowns.

We are ex-MIT founders who have built at startups that have grown from six to nine figures in revenue. We have raised $5M from institutional investors and angels of category-defining businesses (Decagon, Cognition, etc.) and are aggressively hiring an initial founding engineering team!
