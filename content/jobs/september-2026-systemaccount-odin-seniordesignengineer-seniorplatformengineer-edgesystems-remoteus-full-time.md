Odin - Senior Design Engineer, Senior Platform Engineer — Edge Systems - Remote (US) - Full-time

Odin builds workforce visibility and compliance software for critical infrastructure. We’re deployed on 500+ active projects with 100K+ workers, securing more than $100B in construction value.

Senior Design Engineer — Own interfaces from UX and visual design through production React. You’ll establish our design language and design system, prototype interactions, and ship responsive, accessible frontend experiences. We need someone who genuinely designs and codes.

Senior Platform Engineer, Edge Systems — Build software connecting cameras, access-control readers, rugged devices, and local compute to our cloud across unreliable networks. Work includes protocols, real-time streams, buffering and synchronization, remote deployment, diagnosis, and recovery.

Out stack: TypeScript, Node.js, GraphQL, Postgres, Kubernetes, GCP, React, React Native.

Both roles are US-remote with periodic jobsite travel. US work authorization is required.
