Encube - ML Engineer, ML Ops Engineer - Stockholm, Sweden - ONSITE - Full-time

Encube is a platform where hardware teams look at their 3D designs and engineering drawings together and make better products faster. We&#x27;re building AI that understands those drawings and 3D models, and can eventually create them. Evaluations are a massive priority, and we need to consistently demonstrate how our models are better than frontier VLMs.

ML Engineer: train models that connect 2D drawings to 3D models, build agents that generate CAD and drawings, figure out how to measure if they&#x27;re actually good, and ship them to real customers. PyTorch. Bonus if you&#x27;ve worked with 3D, CAD or graph neural nets.

ML Ops Engineer: on-prem and AWS GPUs, make every experiment reproducible, keep costs down and customer data safe, and get rid of whatever slows the ML team down. SkyPilot, MLflow, PyTorch.
