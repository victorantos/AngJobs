Aloe - AI Research + AI Engineering roles - ONSITE Vancouver, BC, Canada - Full-time

Aloe is the AI lab for justified belief. We work on research and products that bring epistemic intelligence to AI – compound systems that know why they believe what they do, and what would cause that belief to change. Aloe learns how humans come to answers we rely on: recognizing what&#x27;s at stake in a decision, understanding evidence and unknowns, working through hard questions with rigor, and learning from the sparse data of experience.

Please read the job description and include in your outreach why you fit not just the role but also our culture. We strive to be Level 5 humans, co-creating a deliberately developmental atmosphere. We get a lot of applications – showing your personality will help you stand out. We have to want to work with you, after all!

The future we’re building for:

- Commoditize access to expertise, capability, and AI’s economic promise for both businesses and end-users

- Make new discoveries, products, and experiences possible at speeds unbound from human performance

- Defend against negligent or malicious use of AI with internal governance and risk evaluation at the foundation

- Invert the extraction economy with AI that respects human cognition instead of exploiting it
