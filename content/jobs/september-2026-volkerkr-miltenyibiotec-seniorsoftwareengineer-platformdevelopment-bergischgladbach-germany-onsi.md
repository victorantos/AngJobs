Miltenyi Biotec - (Senior) Software Engineer – Platform Development - Bergisch Gladbach, Germany - ONSITE or PARTIALLY REMOTE - Full-time

For 35 years, Miltenyi Biotec has been developing innovative medical devices to empower biomedical research and advance medical treatments. This is how we are pushing the boundaries of cell and gene therapy and personalized medicine.  To realize our vision, we combine sophisticated research with advanced engineering solutions in agile software development, data science, internet of things, artificial intelligence and connectivity. Today, more than 4,800 experts from over 50 countries contribute to breakthrough innovations and sustainable success.

We are looking for a (Senior) Software Engineer in Platform Development. You will contribute to the development and enhancement of our company-wide software platform, playing a key role in implementing a state-of-the-art technology platform for software development in our medical GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) and RUO (Research Use Only) instruments. In this role, you will take on significant technical responsibilities and act as a key point of contact for collaboration with various teams and stakeholders.

- You will contribute to the platform roadmap and its implementation, collaborating with project and program managers, software architects, feature owners, requirements engineers, UX&#x2F;UI designers, developers, and testers. You will drive the platform’s technical advancement and ensure high-quality, on-time delivery.

- You will continuously advance the software platform by developing innovative solutions used across software and instrument products while driving our transition to an AI-first engineering and development approach.

-You will write clean, maintainable, and scalable code in C++ and share your knowledge with the team.

- You will collaborate closely with other departments in our interdisciplinary and international environment, including other software development teams, biologists, IT, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Service, and Marketing.

- You will support communication with external stakeholders, contribute to critical business decisions, and provide input and ideas to shape the future of our global software development organization.
