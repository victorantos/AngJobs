Tasklet - Head of Design Engineering, Software Engineer (multiple), Customer Success Engineer - San Francisco, CA - ONSITE - Full-time

Tasklet is building the AI platform companies use to run their business on agents. You describe an outcome in plain English and our agents do the work across your SaaS tools, APIs, browser, files, and internal systems — immediately, or in the cloud 24x7 on a schedule or in response to events. Every agent gets its own cloud computer, filesystem, database, and browser. We&#x27;re a team of 12 in SF, founded by Andrew Lee (Firebase founder) and Jonny Dimond, backed by $42M from USV, Lightspeed, YC, and others.

Head of Design Engineering ($300–450K base, 0.35–0.70%) — IC role owning our product experience end to end: decide how Tasklet should work, design our UX, and personally ship it in production. Looking for an experienced ex-founder builder with exceptional taste and real design &amp; frontend chops.

Software Engineer — multiple roles ($220–400K base, 0.15–0.60%) — agent harness, context management, sandboxed execution, model serving, storage, Kubernetes, mobile &amp; web apps. Deep systems fundamentals matter more to us than an AI résumé. Mid-level through Staff+.

Customer Success Engineer ($130–180K base, 0.05–0.15%) — own customer relationships &amp; support our users. Help our customers use Tasklet more effectively and feed what you learn back into your core offering. We&#x27;re looking for an AI power-user who&#x27;s great with customers. ~1–5 years professional experience. No engineering background required.

All roles are in person at our office in SF -- no exceptions -- and we&#x27;re not sponsoring visas at this time.
