We The Flywheel - Remote - Brand Identity Designer (AI-agent team) - Hourly, flexible hours

We build and run our own brands (race finders, training tools, AI guides, sports media) with a small remote team and a lot of AI agents.

Most of our brands have an identity that is good enough. We want someone with a sharp eye to make them good: logo refinements, lockups, favicons, social and OG images, colour and type rules. It&#x27;s polish and judgment, not starting from scratch. Our agents do the digging, so you spend your time designing.

Please include identity work that is live somewhere. There&#x27;s a short assessment, read by a person.
