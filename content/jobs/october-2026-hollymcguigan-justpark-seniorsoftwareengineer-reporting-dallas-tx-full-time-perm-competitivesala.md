JustPark is the premier partner offering both B2B solutions for destinations and B2C services for drivers, giving us the best of both worlds. We simplify the entire parking experience. From venues and councils to private driveways, our platform makes it simple for drivers to find, book, and pay for parking—while empowering our operating partners to deliver exceptional parking experiences.

Join the JustPark Reporting team. You&#x27;ll build, ship and maintain the data platform and reporting products behind our venue operations, used at venues like Mercedes Benz Stadium, The Greek Theatre and The O2 Arena. Following the 2024 merger of ParkHub and JustPark, we&#x27;re scaling internationally, which means distributed systems, teams, and interesting problems at the intersection of payments, data and real time event operations. This is a hands-on engineering role where you&#x27;ll own meaningful pieces of our platform from design through production, including keeping them healthy on live event days. 
We&#x27;re an AI native engineering team: engineers use Claude Code, Copilot and Cursor as daily tools for code generation, review, debugging and documentation, and we build MCP integrations that connect our AI tools directly to Jira, Confluence, Slack and internal APIs so engineers stay in flow. 
You&#x27;ll work closely with Product, QA, and our UK engineering and data teams to shape the data strategy of a fast growing international company.

Some of what you&#x27;ll be doing:
-Design, build and ship backend services and streaming data pipelines in Go that handle real world scale
-Own data end to end: event schema and contract design, durable messaging patterns, and the read models and warehouse tables analytics is served from
-Build financial data flows, including payment processor ingestion, fee reconciliation and ledger backed reporting
-Ingest and reconcile third party data (partner files and payment processor APIs) with idempotent, replayable backfills
-Develop and evolve API and GraphQL serving layers that power our internal and external products
-Migrate existing reporting paths onto the new architecture
