TheraPlug - Founding CTO &#x2F; Technical Lead - Detroit preferred; remote considered - Equity-first, pre-funding

I’m Marcelus Brice, founder of TheraPlug (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;theraplug.health" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;theraplug.health</a>). We’re building a behavioral health platform focused on making it easier for patients to find appropriate therapists and access care through insurance, starting in Detroit.

We’re looking for a hands-on technical leader who can build alongside a lean team and help recruit its founding engineers. You don’t need a previous CTO title, but you should have shipped and operated production software.

The product will support iOS, Android and responsive web, including:

• Patient and provider onboarding, scheduling and secure messaging.
• AI-assisted therapist matching based on clinical needs, preferences, insurance and availability.
• Provider credential verification and payer enrollment workflows.
• Insurance eligibility checks and estimated patient costs.
• Secure video sessions and clinician-reviewed AI documentation.

Relevant experience includes full-stack development, mobile apps, healthcare integrations, sensitive-data security and practical AI implementation. We value clear architecture, accessible design and thoughtful use of established services.

Compensation: We are pre-funding and cannot offer an immediate cash salary. We’re seeking someone interested in an equity-first founding role, with a planned transition to salary when funding supports payroll. Equity, responsibilities, time commitment and compensation triggers would be agreed in writing before work begins. Funding timing is not guaranteed.

To start a conversation, email marcelus@theraplug.health with:
• Products you’ve built and your specific contribution.
• Relevant healthcare, security or AI experience.
• Your location and realistic availability before funding.
• Your interest in leading and assembling a small development team.

I can share the detailed product scope and team requirements.
