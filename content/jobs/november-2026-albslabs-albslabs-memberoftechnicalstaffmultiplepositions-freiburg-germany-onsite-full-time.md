Albs Labs - Member of Technical Staff (Multiple Positions) - Freiburg, Germany - ONSITE - Full-time

Albs is an AI research lab building real-time multimodal intelligence for machines, enabling them to see, hear, reason, and interact. We treat model architecture, inference, and runtime as one system. We are hiring several Staff- and Senior-level IC roles and are looking for experienced researchers and engineers, typically with a PhD and several years of research or industry experience, or an equivalent track record. We hire across the following areas:

- LLM Post-Training (RLHF, preference optimization, evals)

- Speech-to-Speech Models (incl. ASR and TTS)

- LLM Pre-Training (incl. model architectures and optimizers)

- Synthetic Data Generation (pipelines, filtering, data quality)
