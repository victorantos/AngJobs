Robotics and AI Institute - Software Engineering Intern - ONSITE (Zürich, Switzerland) - Internship - No visa sponsorship

We&#x27;re hiring some robotics systems software focused interns in our Zürich office. We&#x27;re specifically looking for late stage masters students with strong software engineering fundamentals and some skills in turning math into performant code. We have two tracks available, either offline training infrastructure for RL (physics simulation, gaussian splatting, RL algo implementation) or on-robot deployment (online inference, fast C++).

The software team in Zürich is involved in building the tools that accelerate research, while having a close connection to what the researchers are doing.

We&#x27;re looking for people who already have the right to work in Switzerland. That means Swiss&#x2F;EU&#x2F;EFTA citizens, or those with a work permit in Switzerland already.
