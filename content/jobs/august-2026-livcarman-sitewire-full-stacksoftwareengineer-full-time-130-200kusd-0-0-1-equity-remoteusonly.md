Sitewire - Full-Stack Software Engineer - Full-Time - $130-200k USD - 0-0.1% Equity - Remote (US only)

I&#x27;m the head of engineering at Sitewire, a construction finance platform that helps lenders and real estate investors track projects, automate work verification, and speed up payments. Our small, fully-remote team has built a profitable product that&#x27;s expanded organically 3x per year, mainly through word of mouth. We&#x27;re now looking to roughly double the size of our engineering team as we enter our next phase of growth.

We&#x27;re looking for smart, pragmatic full-stack engineers who operate with a high degree of autonomy and ownership. You don&#x27;t need experience with Rails and it&#x27;s not what we&#x27;re hiring for: we&#x27;re looking for React experience + solid backend engineering experience with any modern language.
