Brightcore Energy - Senior Embedded Software&#x2F;Hardware Engineer - Brooklyn, NY (hybrid, 3 days&#x2F;week onsite) - Full-time - $140k-$170k+ base, flexible for the right experience, plus annual bonus

Brightcore Energy designs, builds, and operates clean energy systems, geothermal, solar, and building energy efficiency, for commercial and institutional buildings. A growing share of our work is managing geothermal cooling assets at data centers.

We&#x27;re hiring an owner for Brightcore EDGE, an on-prem industrial PC that runs our control and optimization algorithms directly at customer sites, including data center facilities where real-time energy management is mission-critical. You&#x27;d select and harden the industrial PC and Linux platform, build the imaging, provisioning, and fleet-update process, integrate with building management systems over BACnet and Modbus (Schneider Electric, Johnson Controls, Honeywell, Siemens), build the local time-series data logging and telemetry pipeline, and implement OS hardening, disk encryption, and network segmentation to satisfy OT security reviews at high-security sites.

Reports directly to our VP of Innovation. Looking for 5-8+ years in embedded systems, industrial controls, or edge&#x2F;IoT, strong Linux, Python and&#x2F;or C&#x2F;C++, and some BACnet&#x2F;Modbus background. It&#x27;s a high-ownership, spec-it-and-build-it role, you&#x27;ll be buying hardware and shipping working prototypes without a lot of process overhead.
