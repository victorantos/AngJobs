Elucid builds FDA-cleared AI software that analyzes cardiac CT scans to characterize plaque and predict heart attack and stroke risk. The world&#x27;s best cardiologists use our output to make treatment decisions for real patients. As such, our platform is regulated as a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) product by the FDA and we operate under HIPAA, ISO 13485, and ISO 27001.

We&#x27;re hiring a Senior Security Engineer to help mature and scale our security engineering program: You&#x27;ll work across application security, cloud infrastructure, and regulatory compliance, contributing to secure SDLC practices, threat modeling, code and design reviews, CI&#x2F;CD security testing, AWS hardening and audit other activities to allow Elucid ship secure code quickly within a regulated SDLC.

We are looking for: 5+ years of experience in security engineering, application security, or a closely related software engineering role with a strong secure coding background, including fluency in cloud infrastructure security, common vulnerability classes (OWASP Top 10 and beyond), authentication, and cryptography fundamentals. Healthcare or regulated-industry experience is a plus, but not a requirement.
