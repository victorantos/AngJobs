Aeolus - DevOps Engineer - San Francisco, CA (onsite) - Full-Time
Aeolus is seeking an exceptional DevOps Engineer to take ownership of the cloud infrastructure and deployment systems that power our mission in applied atmospheric science.

In this role, you&#x27;ll be the technical foundation our scientists, ML engineers, and software engineers build on. At the core of our efforts is an operational physical weather model written in Julia, executed on GPUs. Responsibilities include designing and operating cloud environments, deployment pipeliness and infrastructure-as-code that keep our systems reliable, scalable, and fast.  Our ideal candidate has extensive experience in HPC environments with Slurm management.
