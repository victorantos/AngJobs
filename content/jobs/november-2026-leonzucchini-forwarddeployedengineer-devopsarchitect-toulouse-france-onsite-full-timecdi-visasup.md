Curiosity (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;curiosity.ai" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;curiosity.ai</a>) - Forward Deployed Engineer, DevOps Architect - Toulouse, France - ONSITE - Full-time (CDI) - Visa support

Curiosity Studio builds internal apps and agents from a plain description, on a company&#x27;s connected data and inside its permissions. It runs on premises or in a private cloud, with any LLM. We&#x27;re bootstrapped, profitable and based in Munich. Our customers are large industrial and regulated companies, Airbus among them, and Studio has 30TB+ of data connected in production.

We are starting a team in Toulouse that works at customer sites, in French and English, along with the engineers who build Studio.

Forward Deployed Engineer: take a customer&#x27;s problem from the first workshop to production. You decide with them what Studio should build, connect their systems, model the data as a graph, and oversee the C#&#x2F;.NET connectors and services it needs. You also ship features and agents on top.

DevOps Architect: Own deployments in customer environments, from the first technical discussion to handover, on private clouds or data centers. Networking, IAM, Linux, Docker, monitoring, backups, upgrades, incidents. You also write code alongside the forward deployed engineers.

We&#x27;re looking for hands-on builders who enjoy working directly with customers. Full-stack project experience preferred. Fluent French and English required.

Interviews: a 30 minute call, a technical conversation, then the founders.
