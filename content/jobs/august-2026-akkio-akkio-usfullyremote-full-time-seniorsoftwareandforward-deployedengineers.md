Akkio - US Fully Remote - Full-time - Senior Software and Forward-Deployed Engineers

Akkio is building AI-native applications and agentic workflows for large media agencies and brands: audience building and distribution, measurement &#x2F; campaign analysis, media planning, etc. Generative and traditional AI&#x2F;ML. We have reached &amp; established PMF and are growing our customer base rapidly. See <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.akkio.com" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.akkio.com</a>

Note that we only have two levels at Akkio: Software Engineer and Senior Software Engineer. There is no principal, staff, etc.

* Senior Software Engineer, 160k-240k w&#x2F; equity: Looking for experienced web, backend, and full-stack candidates who have run projects, guided architecture, and built large applications.
