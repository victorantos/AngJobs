Cascade Space - Senior Mechanical Engineer - San Francisco, CA (ONSITE) - Full-time - $150k - $190k + equity

Cascade Space is building ground-based infrastructure to support missions to the moon and beyond. Instead of single large dishes, we&#x27;re building arrays of dishes combined with a digital beamformer into one large aperture — comparable to a NASA Deep Space Network 34m dish. We raised our seed round last year and this is our first mechanical engineering hire.

You&#x27;ll own mechanical integration of vendor dish systems with our in-house RF hardware (feeds, LNAs, amplifiers): packaging for ultra-low-noise electronics that has to survive outdoor conditions, alignment&#x2F;tolerance strategy, and structural&#x2F;environmental testing. When it&#x27;s time to stand up our first array, you&#x27;ll be on-site for deployment and commissioning. Full arc from CAD to field install.

Looking for 4+ years designing and deploying complex hardware systems, experience integrating electronics into outdoor&#x2F;exposed environments, thermal management of sensitive electronics, precision mechanical assemblies, and owning vendor&#x2F;fab-shop relationships. RF&#x2F;antenna experience is a bonus, not required.

Note: US Persons only (ITAR&#x2F;EAR — some customer datasets are restricted).
