ORIGAMICS - Founding Researcher - San Francisco - ONSITE&#x2F;REMOTE

Origamics is building AI models that reason about electronics and help turn schematics into production-ready boards.

We’re looking for a Founding Researcher to work directly with the founders on physics-informed models for Electronics design — physical behavior, simulation, and scientific ML. You’ll tackle open-ended research problems, push models from experiments to production and real hardware, and have the opportunity to publish novel research along the way.

You’ll likely be a good fit if you have strong ML&#x2F;research fundamentals, enjoy 0→1 problems, and have experience with scientific ML, geometric deep learning, numerical methods, simulation, EE, or applied physics. PhD is a plus, not a requirement.
