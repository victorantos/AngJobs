Spanish National Cancer Research Centre (CNIO) - Madrid, Spain - HYBRID - Senior Scientific Computing Engineer - Full-time

CNIO is one of Europe&#x27;s leading cancer research centres. We&#x27;re looking for a senior scientific computing engineer to join the IT, Scientific Computing and AI area and take ownership of the infrastructure behind our research, from genomics pipelines to AI models. You&#x27;ll be the senior scientific computing person in the area, reporting directly to me (the area head), and you&#x27;ll help define where scientific computing at CNIO goes over the next few years.

What you&#x27;ll do:

- Shape the roadmap for scientific computing at the centre and drive it forward

- Own and evolve our HPC cluster (Slurm): scheduling, monitoring, capacity planning, user support

- Build out our GPU &#x2F; AI infrastructure as the centre&#x27;s ML workloads grow

- Work with researchers to turn their analyses into robust, reproducible pipelines and tools

- Optional: co-author research papers

What we&#x27;re looking for:

- Several years running Linux HPC environments in production (Slurm or similar)

- Comfortable with automation (Ansible, Python), containers (Apptainer&#x2F;Docker) and workflow managers (Nextflow, Snakemake or similar)

- Someone who wants to set direction, not just keep the lights on

- Bonus: Ceph, Kubernetes, GPU stacks, a bioinformatics background

- Spanish not required
