Axmed - AI Engineer (Agentic Systems) - REMOTE (preferred CET ±2: Spain, UK, Poland, Romania) - Full-time

Axmed runs a live B2B marketplace connecting healthcare providers in low- and middle-income countries with pharmaceutical suppliers, working to make sure geography and income never stand between someone and the medicine they need. Backed by the Gates Foundation and Founderful. We&#x27;re past proving it works and into scaling it — this role is what takes us there.

You&#x27;ll design and ship autonomous agents that run real steps of the marketplace (sourcing, quoting, order matching, supplier follow-up) on real transactions and real money, with a human in the loop only where risk demands it. Not research: you consume LLMs via API and build the judgment, guardrails, and monitoring around them, end to end, and own what you ship.

Stack: Python, LangChain&#x2F;LangGraph&#x2F;LlamaIndex&#x2F;CrewAI (your choice), LLM APIs, cloud infra, CI&#x2F;CD.

No visa sponsorship&#x2F;relocation.
