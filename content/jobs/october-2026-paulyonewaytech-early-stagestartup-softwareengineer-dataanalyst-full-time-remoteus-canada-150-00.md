For the Data Analyst role, we’re looking for someone who can turn messy data into clear insights. Strong SQL&#x2F;Python skills and the ability to drive product and business decisions with data are key.

Some reasons you may enjoy working with us:

* Collaborative and innovative environment where your voice is heard
* Fully remote with flexible hours
* Long-term, full-time role (not a fixed-term contract)
* High ownership and interesting technical&#x2F;data challenges
* Work with a talented team shipping products customers use every day

Only US and Canadian applicants will be considered.

If you’re interested, send your resume to paulephraimebuka@gmail.com and mention which role you’re applying for.
