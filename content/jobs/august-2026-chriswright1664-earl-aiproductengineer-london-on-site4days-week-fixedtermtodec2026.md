Earl - AI product engineer - London - On-site 4 days&#x2F;week - Fixed term to Dec 2026

We’re hiring an AI-native engineer to work with Earl on a live project inside a Premier League football club. This is not an AI strategy role, and it’s not a chatbot demo job. You’ll work directly with people inside the club&#x27;s commercial team, find valuable problems, and build production AI systems that are actually used.

We’re looking for someone who can move from vague problem → technical design → working product quickly.

You do not need football experience.

You do need strong product judgement, the ability to work directly with non-technical users, and evidence that you’ve actually shipped things.

We’re particularly interested in people who build with AI as their default way of working, rather than conventional engineers who have recently added an LLM API to their CV.

To apply, send me:

- A short intro
- 2-3 things you’ve built
 What you personally did
- How you currently use AI to build software
- Location and availability

Strong candidates will do a paid practical working session.

Contact: chris@earl.partners
