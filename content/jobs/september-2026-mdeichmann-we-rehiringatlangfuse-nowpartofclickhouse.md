We are looking for a Product Manager, Product &amp; Backend Engineers, Product Marketing Manager and Developer Relations Engineers to join us in building the most widely adopted open-source LLM engineering platform.

- Berlin or EU hybrid (Engineering roles) &#x2F; US-fully remote (Product &amp; Marketing roles)

- ~1 week onsite in Berlin every 2 months — travel covered

- Full-time

Right now, we’re focused on helping customers segment their traces, surface insights from them, and ship changes to their agents faster — building observability at serious scale while creating the product surface engineers actually live in day to day.
