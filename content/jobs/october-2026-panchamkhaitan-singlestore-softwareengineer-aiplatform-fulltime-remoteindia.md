SingleStore - Software Engineer, AI Platform - Full Time, Remote India

SingleStore is a distributed SQL database built for high-performance transactional and real-time analytical workloads. As data systems evolve, we’re building the platform for AI applications that operate directly over enterprise data, including retrieval, reasoning, agent workflows, and intelligent automation.

Our AI Platform team works at the intersection of distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and applied AI. Some of the technical problems we’re working on include:

- Agent runtimes, orchestration, and MCP-style tool integration

- Retrieval&#x2F;RAG pipelines, embeddings, and vector search

- Model integrations, evaluation, and observability

- Secure multi-tenant AI infrastructure

- Reliability, latency, cost controls, and scaling AI systems in production

Our stack includes Go, Python, Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, APIs, and modern AI&#x2F;ML tooling.

We’re currently hiring a Software Engineer located in India to join the AI Platform team, along with several other roles across SingleStore.
