We&#x27;re Australia&#x27;s battery-backed electricity company. We fund, install and operate batteries on-site at small businesses, then supply their power at a firm, flat rate 20–40% below incumbents, making the economics work by optimising those batteries against wholesale prices. We&#x27;re live with real customers. Team of six, backed by Systemiq and Black Nova; engineering includes Casey, who previously led Google engineering in Paris and Google Maps in Sydney.

We&#x27;re AI-native: engineers, energy specialists and go-to-market colleagues all ship with coding agents. Each engineer owns a product end to end, from the commercial problem through to production. Flat structure, lots of ownership.

Areas you could own:

* Customer experience: quoting, onboarding and the customer app

* Metering and billing: turning high-volume interval data into accurate bills

* Energy-market and battery data: the market, weather and telemetry pipelines behind our decisions

* The agent-driven development platform the whole team builds on

Stack: TypeScript, React, Postgres, serverless. Intro chat, system design, an agentic build, then a day with the team.
