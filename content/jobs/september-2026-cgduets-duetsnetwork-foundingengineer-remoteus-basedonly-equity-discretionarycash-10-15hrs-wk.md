Duets Network - Founding Engineer - REMOTE (US-Based Only) - Equity + discretionary cash - ~10–15 hrs&#x2F;wk

Duets Network is an AI-powered platform connecting startups and investors across the sports and entertainment industries. We are building a dynamic ecosystem that bridges founders, capital, and strategic partners to drive meaningful growth and collaboration at the intersection of technology and culture.

The initial product is built out, and we&#x27;re now looking for a fractional Founding Engineer to guide the technical side moving forward: architecture, infrastructure, data&#x2F;recommendation systems, security, and scalability.

Our founding team: CEO, ex-Universal Music Group; CBO, ex-Andreessen Horowitz. You&#x27;d work directly with the founders, with no existing engineering org, and would:

- Assess and improve the existing React &#x2F; Node.js &#x2F; PostgreSQL &#x2F; AWS codebase and infrastructure

- Set engineering architecture, standards, and technical priorities

- Design the data + recommendation&#x2F;scoring layer behind founder&#x2F;investor&#x2F;partner matching

- Build out DevOps, CI&#x2F;CD, monitoring, and deployment processes

- Make pragmatic build-vs-buy decisions and coordinate external engineers&#x2F;vendors as needed

- Serve as the primary technical team member, with real influence over the technology foundation and future engineering org

We&#x27;re particularly interested in someone who has owned architecture end-to-end. Ideally with experience in matching, recommendation, ranking, or scoring systems. You should be comfortable explaining technical tradeoffs to non-technical founders and investors.

This is a fractional role (~10–15 hrs&#x2F;wk), compensated with equity plus discretionary cash. It&#x27;s a fit if you want the opportunity to be an early technical team member without a traditional full-time commitment. We&#x27;re early-stage and moving fast, so you don&#x27;t need to check every box, we&#x27;re looking for someone curious, adaptable, and excited to build with us.

If interested, email team[at]duetsnetwork[dot]com with &quot;Founding Engineer&quot; in the subject line.
