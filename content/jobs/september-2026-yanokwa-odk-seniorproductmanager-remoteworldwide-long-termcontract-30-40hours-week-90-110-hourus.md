ODK - Senior Product Manager - Remote (Worldwide) - Long-term contract, 30–40 hours&#x2F;week - $90–$110&#x2F;hour USD

ODK is an open-source platform used by thousands of organizations worldwide to collect billions of data points for disease surveillance, supply chain traceability, environmental monitoring, and more. It enables users to build powerful forms and reliably collect structured data, even in places with little or no Internet connectivity.

ODK has been actively developed for 18 years. We have a mix of self-hosted and SaaS users across many sectors. We&#x27;re looking for a senior product manager who will own product discovery and prioritization across this diverse user base. To be successful, you&#x27;ll need to find the common needs behind very different workflows and turn them into opinionated product improvements.

Because the team is small and the user base is large, this is a high-autonomy role where product decisions have an unusually direct impact. You should have substantial experience managing complex B2B or technical products. Experience with open source, self-hosted software, and small, fully remote teams is a plus.
