Lokker.com - Senior Backend Engineer, Scanning Infrastructure - REMOTE: North America &#x2F; Europe, UTC-8 to UTC+2 - Full-time

Lokker is a privacy platform that helps organizations understand and control what is happening across public-facing infrastructure: websites, browser-side applications, scripts, pixels, cookies, tags, forms, SDK&#x2F;API calls, and browser-side data flows.

We are hiring a senior backend engineer to own and continue to scale Lokker’s scanning infrastructure. This role focuses on backend systems for high-volume website scanning, browser automation, distributed scanning, networking, reliability, performance, and cost optimization.

IMPORTANT: A key requirement for this role is substantial, hands-on production experience with browser automation, headless browsers, crawlers, scanners, synthetic monitoring, scraping infrastructure, or closely related technologies. This is not a general backend role, and we can only consider candidates who have already built, operated, or scaled systems in this domain.

Other relevant experience includes distributed systems, queues&#x2F;orchestration, high-scale backend services, HTTP&#x2F;TLS&#x2F;DNS, proxies, CDNs, WAFs, bot detection, and fingerprinting.

Production experience in Go, Rust, TypeScript&#x2F;Node.js, C++, or similar.
