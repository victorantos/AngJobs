Hardware gets most of the attention, but the algorithms and the tooling around them are where the field actually becomes useful, and today that work is scattered across notebooks and one-off scripts. We host versioned datasets and reproducible benchmarks so results are comparable, and give teams a way to publish a workflow that someone else can pick up and run. Think Kaggle&#x2F;Hugging Face, but for quantum. We&#x27;re a small but growing team, having just raised our seed round, and we&#x27;re now extending into the US.

We&#x27;re looking for one senior full-stack engineer. You&#x27;d own features end to end: GraphQL API and services in Rust, React&#x2F;TypeScript frontend, plus the runner that executes user-submitted workflows against simulators and real backends.

You don&#x27;t need a quantum background, but you do need to enjoy going deep on an unfamiliar technology and getting productive in it fast. That happens here constantly, and we&#x27;ll give you the time and the people to learn it properly. Beyond that: production Rust you&#x27;ve shipped and maintained, and real comfort across the stack.

Onsite in Paris or remote anywhere in the EU. If you&#x27;re not in Paris, expect to join us there regularly for team weeks.
