FitMate - Frontend Engineer (Junior) - REMOTE (US) - $80-120k + bonus

We are building a scalable 1-1 coaching platform to help people achieve their fitness goals. We already have a working nutrition product with a rapidly growing user base and are hoping to branch out into other areas of fitness this quarter.

Our product aims to hook people up with a qualified human coach at an affordable price. We have a client facing mobile app and web app that allows our users to track their nutrition and training, and a coach facing app that allows our coaches to get insights into their clients and communicate with them over SMS or video call.

We&#x27;re a small, lean, mission-driven team and the role is a great opportunity for someone starting out in their career who wants to learn quickly in a fast paced environment.

We use React Native and Expo for our mobile app and Astro and Svelte for our web apps.

* Applicants must be based in the US, we are unable to sponsor visas.
