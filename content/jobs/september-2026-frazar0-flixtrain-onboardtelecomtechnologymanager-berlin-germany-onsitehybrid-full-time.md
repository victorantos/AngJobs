FlixTrain - Onboard Telecom Technology Manager - Berlin, Germany - ONSITE (hybrid) - Full-time

FlixTrain is on a mission to make long-distance rail travel affordable and sustainable across Europe. Every passenger we shift to rail matters, and the onboard connectivity is a key part of what makes people switch.

We&#x27;re hiring an Onboard Technology Manager to own everything that keeps passengers connected and crew productive on the train: Wi-Fi and network infrastructure, onboard units, and mobile devices for train staff. As satellite constellations and 5G redefine what&#x27;s possible for high-speed mobile connectivity, you&#x27;ll help set the direction for the technology being adopted on the largest non-governmental train fleet: writing specs, defining network architectures, monitoring live performance, and driving improvement projects from their conception to the finish line.

The role is highly cross-functional. You&#x27;ll work with internal partners (operations, customer product, and rail engineering) and external ones, such as tech vendors (Icomera and Advantech) and train manufacturers (Siemens, Talgo).

What we&#x27;re looking for:

- Bachelor&#x27;s or Master&#x27;s in Telecommunications, CS, Electrical Engineering, or similar

- 2+ years hands-on with network architecture, Wi-Fi systems, or Mobile Device Management (ideally in rail or a similarly complex environment)

- Strong troubleshooting skills on complex networked systems

- Comfortable leading cross-functional initiatives and communicating clearly with both engineers and non-technical stakeholders

- Fluent German and English (internally, English is preferred, but German is often mandatory when working with suppliers and regulatory bodies)

Perks: hybrid office&#x2F;remote work model, up to 60 extra remote days&#x2F;year from abroad (work from anywhere), 12 free FlixTrain travel vouchers&#x2F;year + 12 for friends and family, mental health support, L&amp;D budget, mentoring program.
