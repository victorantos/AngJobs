K2 Space - k2space.com - Torrance, CA OR Seattle, WA - ONSITE - Full Time

K2 is building the largest and highest-power satellites ever flown, unlocking performance levels previously out of reach across every orbit. The rise of heavy-lift launch vehicles is shifting the industry from an era of mass constraint to one of mass abundance, and we believe this new era demands a fundamentally different class of spacecraft.
The software team at K2 is building our stack primarily in Rust. You will be able to write mission-critical code that controls and tests propulsion systems, attitude control systems, and power systems to ensure safe and reliable operation of the vehicle.
