Tahoma AI - tahoma.io - Austin, TX or Seattle, WA (ONSITE optional) - REMOTE (US) - Full-time - Founding Engineer

Tahoma builds AI agents that automate healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM): eligibility, claim status, claim submission, denials, and payment posting. We&#x27;re live in production with paying enterprise customers.

Our agents operate payer portals behind MFA, green-screen terminals over SSH, and Windows desktop software, where some actions are irreversible. They run on a deterministic orchestration layer we built in-house, with model calls as bounded steps, not the control loop.

Stack: TypeScript&#x2F;Node.js, Playwright, computer-use agents, Claude, Postgres, Azure, K8s.

You&#x27;ll get to own the core platform end to end and make the real architecture calls: resilience to portal changes, exactly-once guarantees, observability, and cost per run.

Team: Founder from AWS, the former UnitedHealthcare CIO,  advisors from Microsoft Research and Shopify&#x27;s AI team, and a remote engineering team in-flight.
