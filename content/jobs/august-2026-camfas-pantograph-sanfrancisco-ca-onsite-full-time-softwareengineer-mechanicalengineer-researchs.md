Pantograph - San Francisco, CA - ONSITE - Full-time - Software Engineer, Mechanical Engineer, Research Scientist

Pantograph is a research lab developing generally intelligent robots with simple, scalable methods. We design and manufacture our robots in-house — we published full details on our robot today and opened the waitlist. It&#x27;s an open, hackable robot: a full NixOS machine that runs any model, starting at about 4K. We&#x27;ll be building thousands of them as we aim to collect 10M hours of on-embodiment robot data for our models.

We&#x27;re a public benefit corporation. We care about transparency and openness, and we think a lot about what it would look like for robotics to go well. Some of our thinking on mission and impact is on our website: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;pantograph.com&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;pantograph.com&#x2F;</a>

We&#x27;re hiring across the hardware and ML research teams. We&#x27;re looking for a great generalist SWE to work with our hardware team on our robot feature stack and improve our customer experience (Rust and Python, no robotics background required), a mechanical design engineer to take our first-generation robots from prototype through large-scale production, and research scientists with experience in large-scale pre-training, self-supervised RL, or video modeling.

Open roles: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;pantograph.com&#x2F;careers" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;pantograph.com&#x2F;careers</a>

If you don&#x27;t see the perfect role but you&#x27;re excited about what we&#x27;re doing, email hiring@pantograph.com with your background and what you&#x27;d want to do here.
