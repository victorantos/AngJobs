AusRehab is a workplace rehabilitation provider in Australia — we help injured workers in the NSW workers&#x27; compensation scheme return to work. We&#x27;re replacing a stack of off-the-shelf systems with in-house, AI-driven ones, so our consultants can do their work better: fewer errors, statutory deadlines met, clearer communication with workers and insurers, and the audit and regulatory requirements handled properly rather than remembered at the last minute.

For this we&#x27;re assembling a small team of 3-4 full-stack developers who will work directly with our rehab consultants to design systems that actually fit the task. You&#x27;d own solutions end to end, with real autonomy and no ops team to hand your infrastructure to. You&#x27;d also talk to the people using what you build.

Stack: Python, TypeScript, React, React Native, PostgreSQL, AWS (Lambda&#x2F;S3&#x2F;SQS&#x2F;EC2), GitHub Actions.

Prior experience with ML, NLP, and agentic AI systems is highly desirable, but not essential.

No workers&#x27; comp knowledge needed, but we do ask for a curious mind to learn from an unfamiliar domain.
