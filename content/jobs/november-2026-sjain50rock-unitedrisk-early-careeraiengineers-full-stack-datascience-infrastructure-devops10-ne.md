Our in-house AI team builds the tools that make specialist underwriters quicker and smarter: submission ingestion, underwriting workbenches, risk analysis and back-office automation.

All roles: 0–4 years of experience (internships and co-ops count; new grads and junior engineers welcome), strong Python, working SQL, and something you built that you can show us: GitHub, a deployed app or a portfolio. Each role leans one way, but everyone works across apps, data and infrastructure, and three senior AI engineers review your work. Start dates from December 2026.
