Qualcomm - Canada - Full Time - Engineer - Remote(Canada)- QEMU

My team is looking to hire a ~entry-level software developer to help us design and extend QEMU support [1] for the Hexagon DSP [2]. We&#x27;re looking for candidates who have experience coding in C, C++ or Rust* and who aren&#x27;t afraid of rolling up their sleeves and poring over disassembled code. This particular architecture is a bit more challenging than most, because it&#x27;s a bit unique. It&#x27;s a VLIW [3], so it&#x27;s a great opportunity to learn about an interesting architecture. Millions of Hexagon DSPs are out there, most are found in DragonWing&#x2F;Snapdragon&#x2F;DragonFly SoCs for Laptops, Android phones, cars and more!

We collaborate a lot with Open Source communities like QEMU, LLVM [4], the Linux kernel [5], and more.  I love being able to work on Open Source software at my day job.

You can contact me directly (email addr is in my HN user link). If you don&#x27;t have experience with QEMU - don&#x27;t worry!  Most of us didn&#x27;t have any experience with it until recently, too.

The rest of the team you&#x27;d work with lives mostly in Canada, EU, US.

* Rust experience may serve the role well but we don’t (yet) have any product software in Rust.
