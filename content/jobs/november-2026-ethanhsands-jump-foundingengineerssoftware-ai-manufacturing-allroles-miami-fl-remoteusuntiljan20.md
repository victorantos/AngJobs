Ethan here, founder of Jump.

Machine shops lose jobs because quoting is slow. A part comes in, someone has to read the drawing, figure out how to make it, and price it, and that can take hours. Many shops also can&#x27;t put customer drawings in the cloud (ITAR, export control, or just customers who say no).

We&#x27;re building Jump Quote: AI that reads a shop&#x27;s drawings and drafts quotes in minutes, running on the shop&#x27;s own computer. We&#x27;re starting with a small group of founding CNC shops. Long term, we want anyone to be able to make anything, anywhere.

We&#x27;re hiring founding engineers first (software&#x2F;AI and manufacturing), but every role is open. If you&#x27;re exceptional at something a startup needs, apply.

We care about kindness, honesty, ambition and intelligence. We don&#x27;t care where you went to school or who you&#x27;ve worked for. We care that you have a winning mindset and can learn and ship fast.

How it works: we start now, remote within the US. In January 2027 we&#x27;re together in person in Miami, and that&#x27;s when the $140K–$160K + equity package starts. You need to be authorized to work in the US for at least the next 3 years; we can&#x27;t sponsor visas.
