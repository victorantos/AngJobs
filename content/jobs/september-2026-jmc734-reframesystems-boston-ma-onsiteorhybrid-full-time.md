Reframe Systems - Boston, MA - ONSITE or HYBRID - Full-time

We&#x27;re creating housing on a mass scale through a network of robotic microfactories producing highly-customized buildings, from ADUs and cabins through townhomes and mid-rise apartments. We build with industrial efficiency while making buildings for an exact footprint, scale, style, and level of finish to fit any market, regardless of how tight the site or the zoning.

The core software problem is producing buildings designs without the typical months of design development and engineering. You can design natively in our building system or import existing designs, taking in a high-level architectural schematic and using that to generate a detailed manufacturable 3D model of the building (every stud, sheet of OSB, nail, electrical outlet, etc.) used to drive professional engineer review, permitting, and production. A compiler for a building.
