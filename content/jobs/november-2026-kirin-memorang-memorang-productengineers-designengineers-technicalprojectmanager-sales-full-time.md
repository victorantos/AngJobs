Memorang - Product Engineers + Design Engineers + Technical Project Manager + Sales Engineer - Full-Time - US Based Candidates Only

Memorang is an &quot;AI stack for education&quot; that helps organizations build, launch, and monetize AI-powered learning and assessment products. Think Vercel plus Shopify for EdTech. We currently operate on an enterprise model, serving as the AI technology and commercial partner behind new product lines for organizations like ETS, McGraw-Hill, Pearson, and the ACT.

We’re hiring for two roles:

Product Engineers
Own features from the first rough idea to production. You’ll work across AI workflows, backend systems, and user interfaces, making product decisions along the way. We’re looking for engineers who ask good questions, care about the details users notice, and can turn an ambiguous problem into something useful and shipped.

Stack: TypeScript, React&#x2F;Next.js, React Native, Node.js, AWS Lambda, Postgres, and Neo4j.

Design Engineers
Design and build interfaces that make complex workflows feel simple. You’ll take ideas from early exploration through working prototypes to production, with attention to interaction design, typography, accessibility, and the small details that make a product feel finished. We’re looking for someone with strong visual judgment who can also ship in React and TypeScript.

Show us products you’ve designed and built, with a clear sense of what you personally owned.

Technical Project Manager
Turn complex work into clear plans and keep delivery moving. You’ll coordinate across engineering, design, and customer needs, surface dependencies early, resolve blockers, and keep owners and timelines clear as priorities change.

Ideal background: an engineer who moved into project management, with startup experience and enough technical depth to discuss APIs, architecture, and AI workflows with the people building them.

Sales Engineer
Help prospective and current customers understand what they can build with Memorang. You’ll lead technical discovery, build tailored demos and proofs of concept, and work with engineering to scope integrations and solutions around customer needs.

We’re looking for someone who can write code, work with APIs and SDKs, and explain how AI workflows and system architecture fit together. Ideal experience includes developer tools, APIs, cloud infrastructure, or AI platforms, with hands-on work helping customers evaluate and build on those technologies.

Why join?
A small team, meaningful ownership, and products used by millions. You’ll help shape what we build and how we work, with a short path from an idea to seeing it in users’ hands.

Remote-first, async-friendly, with health and wellness stipends, home-office support, and paid time off.

Apply: Email careers@memorang.com with your CV, a few sentences about what interests you, and something you’re proud to have built or delivered.
