Prior Labs - Berlin, Freiburg, NYC - ONSITE - Full-time - <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;priorlabs.ai" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;priorlabs.ai</a>

We build foundation models for tables. TabPFN takes your whole dataset as context and predicts in one forward pass - no training, no tuning - after pre-training only on synthetic data from causal priors. TabPFN v2 was a Nature cover; the open models have 4M+ downloads and run in production from liquid biopsy to rail maintenance. Since July we&#x27;re an independent lab inside SAP with €1B+ over four years; models stay open-weight and research stays public.

We&#x27;re hiring engineers:

Infrastructure Engineer - multi-cluster GPU training on Slurm, GCP today and more providers in 2027. The compute budget is tens of millions a year, and you own it.

ML Engineer, Backend - own inference end to end: latency, batching and memory on million-row inputs, across our API, customer VPCs and open weights.

Full Stack Developer - the product where data scientists, agents and our models meet. TypeScript&#x2F;React + Python&#x2F;FastAPI.

Also hiring: Research Scientists and Research Engineers (Foundation Model), Applied Scientist (NYC), PhD research interns, GTM and ops roles. We sponsor visas and help with relocation.

~45 people, led by Frank Hutter, advised by Yann LeCun and Bernhard Schölkopf.
