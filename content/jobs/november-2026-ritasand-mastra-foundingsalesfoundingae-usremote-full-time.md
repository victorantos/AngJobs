Mastra - Founding Sales (Founding AE) - US Remote - Full-time

Mastra is the open-source TypeScript framework for building AI agents (agents, workflows, memory, evals, tracing). From the team behind Gatsby, YC-backed, <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;mastra-ai&#x2F;mastra" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;mastra-ai&#x2F;mastra</a>

Why: Four months ago Mastra was a very popular open-source agent framework with very little monetization. Then we launched our platform, and now we&#x27;re closing a couple of deals a week. Pipeline is growing faster than one person can work it, so this is our second dedicated sales hire. You start day 1 with a warm pipe: most leads are already using the open-source framework.

What you&#x27;d sell: Platform (Studio, Server, infra to run&#x2F;debug&#x2F;observe agents in production, hosted or self-hosted for regulated orgs), Agent Builder (agents without code), and Concierge (embedded Mastra engineers working alongside customer teams). Full-cycle, mid-market: Series A–C, often with regulatory or infra constraints, sales cycles of a few weeks, talking to eng leaders.

You have: Closed full-cycle deals in dev tools, OSS, infra, or AI. Enough technical depth to come across as a smart PM with engineers: understand their use cases and scope a solution. Founder or founder mentality. Comfort with a product that ships something new every week.
