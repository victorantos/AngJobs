Machine Age - COO — founder-level role - Full-time - UK time zone - Salary post-raise

Machine Age is building a network of businesses and robotics providers to supply real-world data for robot training.

We’re seeing strong interest, with multimillion-dollar opportunities in the pipeline. We’re looking for a COO to work alongside the founder and help turn that interest into delivery.

You’ll grow our network of businesses, bring new robotics providers on board, and build the operations needed to scale both sides. This is a hands-on role for someone who wants to roll up their sleeves, build relationships and get things done.

You’ll need to work UK hours. This is a full-time opportunity, with salary starting after we complete our raise. An interest in robotics is a bonus.

Email frank@machineage.ai with things you’ve done and how you’d approach building a network of businesses and robot suppliers.
