ORFF Maestra - Prototype Developer; Technical Product Lead - REMOTE (US only) - Contract, part-time - <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;orffmaestra.com" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;orffmaestra.com</a>

We&#x27;re preparing a U.S. Department of Education SBIR Phase IA proposal to build an assessment reasoning platform for school speech-language pathologists. It implements TTL-MSM, a fully specified decision framework for one of the hardest calls in school eligibility evaluations: whether a student&#x27;s communication profile reflects a disorder or a difference. Clinicians code each source of evidence, and a fixed rule combines them. The rules are written; the software isn&#x27;t. The research lead is at Baylor University.

Prototype Developer ($70–95&#x2F;hr): build a browser-based web app from a written spec, with privacy designed in from the start so it&#x27;s ready for FERPA-protected student data later. About 15–20 hrs&#x2F;week for nine months if funded.

Technical Product Lead ($75–100&#x2F;hr): the bridge between the clinical spec and the engineering. Scope the prototype, turn the spec into requirements, and keep the build on schedule. About 5–8 hrs&#x2F;week for nine months if funded.

Pre-award scoping (10–15 hrs for the developer, 5–10 for the lead) is unpaid, and funded work is contingent on award. U.S. citizens or permanent residents.
