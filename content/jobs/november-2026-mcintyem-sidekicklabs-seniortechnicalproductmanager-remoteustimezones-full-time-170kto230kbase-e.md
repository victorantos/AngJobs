Sidekick Labs - Senior Technical Product Manager - REMOTE (US Timezones) - Full-time - $170k to $230k Base + Equity + Health

Sidekick builds AI security software for enterprises. Our sensors observe AI behavior and use in situ. From those observations, our reasoning pipeline produces an application inventory and a risk picture, allowing us to provide real-time intervention and coaching right when it matters.

Compensation: base salary scaled to experience, meaningful early-stage equity, and 100% covered health insurance for you and your immediate family.

We are a highly senior team. Most of us have been founders or on early founding teams, so we operate with high ownership. The PM team is small, and everyone on it has a real, current AI-assisted practice: we design, prototype, and build with AI tools every day.

The role: You&#x27;ll spend your time with customers and in the product. You&#x27;ll run discovery with CISOs and IT teams, guide pilots, and turn what you learn into clear direction for engineering. You&#x27;ll also build: design and prototype with AI tools, make your own tools for research, analysis, and reporting, and open PRs against the product when that&#x27;s the fastest way to get something done. On a small team, expect to take on whatever the company needs.

What we&#x27;re looking for:

- 10+ years building B2B software, at least 5 of them as a PM

- Customer discovery and pilots you ran yourself, ideally with enterprise IT or security teams

- Things you built yourself that you can walk us through: prototypes, internal tools, or merged PRs

- Crisp, clear communication, written and spoken, with customers, engineers, and executives

- Security experience is a plus

To apply: Send an email to hiring@sidekicklabs.com with &quot;Senior Technical Product Manager&quot; in the subject. Include a PDF of your resume, links to your LinkedIn and any GitHub or personal site, and a short description of something you built yourself (a prototype, a tool, a PR): what it does and why you built it. Include a link if you can share one.
