Marker Learning - Senior Full-Stack Engineer (Frontend-Leaning) - Full-time - Remote, US only - $180k–$200k - <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.markerlearning.com" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.markerlearning.com</a>

We’re building the Marker Method, a digital assessment platform that helps clinicians understand how students learn. It brings together interactive assessments, automated scoring, and tools for interpreting results. Our norming study is underway, and we’re building toward launch.

We’re looking for a frontend-leaning full-stack engineer to join our small engineering team. You’ll build student-facing assessment experiences and clinician tools, with ownership across the frontend and the systems behind it.

Strong TypeScript, React, and Next.js experience is especially relevant. We care about product judgment, thoughtful UI, and the ability to take an ambiguous problem through to working software. You should be comfortable working independently, making sensible technical decisions, and writing code every day.

If you enjoy getting the details right on software that students and clinicians actually use, we’d love to talk.

Apply: [careers@markerlearning.com] with a résumé or LinkedIn profile and a little about something you’ve built.
