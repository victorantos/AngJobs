Senzing (entity resolution SDK) - Platform Engineer - Remote (USA) - Full-time

Senzing builds enterprise entity-resolution SDK technology — an embeddable library that runs entirely inside customer infrastructure with zero data exfiltration. We&#x27;re hiring a platform engineer to own the AWS infrastructure behind Placekey (our location-data subsidiary), a production API service running at scale.

Day one, this is a cloud&#x2F;platform role: you&#x27;d own the operational health of Placekey&#x27;s stack, close out post-migration hardening, and have real input on where the infrastructure goes next. Over time the role grows to include internal IT and systems work (Active Directory, endpoint&#x2F;laptop provisioning, Tailscale, security) — so we&#x27;re looking for someone who&#x27;s happy owning things end to end, not only the glamorous layer.

Stack: EKS Fargate, Aurora PostgreSQL, S3, Lambda, Cloudflare, API gateway (Kong&#x2F;AWS), Terraform, Grafana&#x2F;CloudWatch&#x2F;Datadog. Apps are Scala and Rust.

Looking for: 5+ years platform&#x2F;cloud infra, production Kubernetes (EKS ideal), managed Postgres (backup&#x2F;recovery&#x2F;tuning), IaC, solid networking, strong Python&#x2F;Bash. You don&#x27;t need to be an expert on the IT&#x2F;systems side today — but you should want to grow into it.

Remote anywhere in the USA. Competitive salary, full health&#x2F;dental&#x2F;vision, 401(k) match, flexible PTO.

To apply: email enghiring@senzing.com with your resume and a short note — tell us about a challenging production system you&#x27;ve owned end to end, including an incident where your monitoring missed something and what you changed afterward.
